How to play

  • Move: stick under your left thumb · WASD
  • Look: drag the right side · mouse
  • Run: hold RUN · Shift (uses stamina)
  • Tap: tap an object in the middle of the view, or TAP · E / click
  • Use item: USE · F / right-click · pick a slot at the top
  • Look behind: hold ⟲ · C
  • JD hears every noise: doors, running, wrong answers
  • BSODA pushes things · Alarm lures JD · Tape deafens JD
  • Scissors cut Playtime's rope · Boots beat Gotta Sweep
  • Quarter: pay the Bully or the vending machine
  • Apple: JD stops to eat · Lock / NoSquee / Zesty / Keys
